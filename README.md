## Research questions

1. How are runoff quantity and drainage hydraulics represented in SWMM applications?
2. How are pollutant buildup, washoff, EMC and first-flush behaviour modelled and validated?
3. What calibration, validation, sensitivity and uncertainty practices support credible predictions?
4. How do GIS resolution, land cover and drainage connectivity affect model representation?
5. What evidence supports GI/LID scenarios and their water-quality outcomes?
6. What data are required to adapt an integrated framework to a monsoon-influenced campus catchment in Bhopal?

## Research integrity

- Do not invent field observations, model outputs, calibration statistics or citations.
- Distinguish measured outcomes from simulated scenarios and conceptual proposals.
- Verify every DOI and full author list against publisher records.
- Mark abstract-only studies separately from full-text-appraised studies.
- Report review screening counts only after the searches and screening are completed.
- Use PRISMA 2020 as a reporting guideline if a systematic review is actually conducted.
